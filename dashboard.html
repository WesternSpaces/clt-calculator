Equity over time

What is a community land trust? You own the home but lease the land from a nonprofit trust for $25 per month. The 99-year lease is renewable and inheritable. This separates the cost of land from the cost of the home, making the purchase price much lower.
How does the resale formula work? When you sell, the home is appraised at current market value. You keep 25% of the appreciation since you bought it, plus all the equity from paying down your mortgage. The trust retains 75% of appreciation to keep the next sale affordable. A lower purchase price today, in exchange for sharing the upside later.

Assumptions

Mortgage rate: 6.5% Current market rate for a 30-year fixed mortgage. Some CHFA programs offer lower rates for qualifying buyers.

HOA/COA dues: $200/month (estimate) Based on comparable CLT developments in Chaffee County. Actual fees vary by development and cover common area maintenance, insurance, and reserves.
Home appreciation: 3% per year (estimate) Your share is based on actual appraised value at time of sale, not a fixed rate. 3% is a conservative long-term estimate for Chaffee County.
Market appreciation: 4% per year Conservative long-term average. Chaffee County saw 41% growth from 2020 to 2022, but the market has since cooled.
Rent inflation: 3% per year Based on historical Chaffee County rent trends. Actual increases may be higher or lower.
Savings interest: 4.5% APY Assumes a high-yield savings account for the renter's down payment fund.
Non-housing expenses: 55% of income Estimated basic living costs (food, transport, health, etc.) beyond housing. Remaining income after rent goes to savings.
Qualifying ratio: 35% Maximum share of gross income a lender will allow for monthly housing costs.
