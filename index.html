What is a community land trust? You own the home but lease the land from a nonprofit trust for $25 per month. The 99-year lease is renewable and inheritable. Separating land from the home is what makes the price affordable.
What about when you sell? The home is appraised at current market value. You keep 25% of the appreciation since you bought it, plus all the equity from paying down your mortgage. The trust retains 75% of appreciation to keep the next sale affordable. A lower purchase price today, in exchange for sharing the upside later.
